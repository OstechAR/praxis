How to use this

This is a model you can move through at your own speed. Nothing plays until you ask it to.

Moving through the story

  • Next and BackMove one step at a time. On a keyboard use → and ←.
  • The chapter barThe eight segments above the buttons are the eight chapters. Tap any segment to jump to the start of that chapter.
  • Pause and ReplayPause freezes the scene where it is. Replay runs the current step again from the start. Space also pauses.

Exploring the scene

  • Drag to look aroundDrag anywhere on the scene to swing the camera around what you are looking at. It settles back when you move to the next step.
  • Tap anything to look closerTap a building, a policy, a switch or a stack of blocks to fly to it and read what it is. Press Return to the story to pick up where you left off.
  • MapShows the whole world you are in, with every station named. M on a keyboard.

Making changes yourself

  • The blue ring and the blue buttonWhen a blue ring appears in the scene, the next change is yours to make. Tap inside the ring, or press the blue button below. Both do the same thing.

Reading the model

  • Gold is moneyGold plates show amounts. One gold block is $10,000. An outlined block is $10,000 the household needs but doesn't have.
  • Blue is information and flowA lit blue line means money or cover is reaching the household. A dark line means it isn't. A dim line means only under certain terms.
  • Orange is fireOnly used in the house fire.

What this is, and isn't

Praxis explains how insurance generally works. Every amount is an illustration in no particular currency. Policies, laws and public programs differ by country and by insurer, and the contract decides what is paid. This is education, not advice about what to buy.
