How to use this

This is a model you can move through at your own speed. Nothing plays until you ask it to.

Moving through the story

  • Next and BackMove one step at a time. On a keyboard use → and ←.
  • The chapter barThe ten segments above the buttons are the ten chapters. Tap any segment to jump to the start of that chapter.
  • Pause and ReplayPause freezes the scene where it is. Replay runs the current step again from the start. Space also pauses.

Exploring the scene

  • Drag to look aroundDrag anywhere on the scene with a finger or the mouse to swing the camera around what you are looking at. It settles back when you move to the next step.
  • Tap anything to look closerTap a building, a stack of blocks or a group of people to fly to it and read what it is. Press Return to the story to pick up where you left off.
  • MapShows the whole world you are in, with every station named. M on a keyboard.

Making changes yourself

  • The blue ring and the blue buttonWhen a blue ring appears in the scene, the next change is yours to make. Tap inside the ring, or press the blue button below. Both do the same thing.

Reading the model

  • Gold is moneyGold plates show prices. In the gold stacks, one block is fifty cents.
  • Blue is informationBlue lines show what is connected. In the town, each blue column shows how far a price has moved from where it started. The white square marks the starting level.
  • Orange means in useA lit oven or stove is working. A dark one is spare.
  • VoiceReads each caption aloud using your device's built-in voice, where the device allows it.

Standards log

What this piece does against the current Praxis standards and SOPs, as read on 2026-10-08. For the production team, not for learners.

Standards this prototype breaks

  • Artstyle Standard §14 · Content-to-Blender SOPNo Blender in the pipeline.The standard workflow is script, storyboard, production spec, Blender Python, preview, render. This piece is drawn live in the browser with WebGL. Nothing is rendered ahead of time, which is what makes back, forward, pause and replay possible.
  • Visual Grammar §2, §14 · Artstyle §23.6Assets are hand-built in web code, not taken from the Asset Library.Every model here is a web-local rebuild with no PRX asset ID. The person is rebuilt to the measurements of the figure used in S001 to S003, which the CEO made the official Praxis person on 2026-10-08. The Asset Catalog and the PRX-CHAR-0001 spec do not record that yet.
  • Visual Grammar §12Custom assets were not reviewed before use.All geometry should be treated as CUSTOM_REQUIRED and unapproved: farm, bakery, ovens, energy tank, trucks, stores, shelf, loaf, price plates, block stacks, the six town stalls, the restaurant and the block-letter title.
  • Artstyle §13, §23.5 · Content-to-Blender core ruleNo approved script, storyboard or production handoff.All ten chapters of step copy, every camera pose and every layout were written during the build. All on-screen text is draft and is not approved content.
  • PA001 Activation Record, Required RedesignBuilt before the interaction architecture was approved.The record asks for the progression model, controls and reveal logic to be defined before implementation. This build is the proposal for that architecture, not an implementation of an approved one.
  • PA001 Activation Record, Preserved InputsThe ten-act L001 story architecture was reused as the chapter structure.The record keeps that document as historical input only. Its acts, its rewind and replay devices and its closing transfer were adapted here because no replacement exists yet.
  • L001 Story Architecture, Act 1 (locked direction)The title reveal has no narrator cut-off and no music swell.The pull-back and the physical title are kept. The title is built from blocks because the page cannot load a 3D typeface.
  • Artstyle §23.8 · Visual Grammar §15No automated style validation.No validator exists for web scenes, so none was run.
  • Artstyle §9, Sonic Identity extensionNo recorded narration and no music.The learner sets the timing and short on-screen text carries the explanation. The Voice button reads the captions with the browser's built-in voice as a stand-in. It is not the Praxis narrator.
  • Artstyle §9, camera directionThe camera is not fully authored.The learner can drag to orbit and can jump to any station at any time.

Extensions that stay inside the rules

  • Artstyle §3Three extra colours, each with one meaning.Ember orange marks an oven or stove in use. Bread brown and wheat tan identify objects. None replaces gold, blue or white.
  • Cross-product semantics, block primitiveGold blocks are money at a fixed scale.In the cost, price and price-over-time stacks one block is $0.50. Blue columns in the town are information: each shows a price relative to where it started, exaggerated eight times so small changes are visible.
  • Artstyle §15Interaction happens inside the 3D scene.The standard allows 2D web UI for interactive blocks. The 3D requirement goes further and should be written in as a new rule for Paced Animation.
  • Research & Scope RecordEvery number is illustrative.All prices, costs and percentage changes are model values chosen to make the mechanism visible. No historical or sourced claim is made. Interest rates, exchange rates, CPI construction and the other deferred topics are left out.

Followed

  • Black stage, semantic colour.Pure black void and no ground plane. Gold appears only on money. Blue marks flows, information and things you can tap.
  • Praxis person.The figure from S001, reused in S002 and S003: rounded-box torso 0.57 by 0.43 by 0.95, sphere head of radius 0.32 seated into it, all white, no limbs or face. Drawn at 0.85 scale. Official Praxis person by CEO decision, 2026-10-08.
  • Miniature model look.Shiny plastic, primitive-led geometry, cutaway buildings with front walls and roofs removed.
  • Spatial narrative.Persistent worlds that are revisited. System, story and detail views. The camera does most of the moving, and objects move only when the motion carries meaning.
